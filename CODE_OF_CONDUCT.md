# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

MorphCSS Community aims to provide a focused, respectful, and useful environment. Participants are expected to:

- communicate constructively and assume good faith;
- discuss ideas and technical work without personal attacks;
- respect differences in experience, identity, background, and perspective;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, and deliberate disruption;
- protect private information and never publish another person's personal data without permission; and
- follow repository policies and applicable law.

## Scope

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other official MorphCSS Community spaces. It also applies when someone publicly represents the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct is harmful or disruptive.

Report conduct concerns privately to [hello@morphcss.site](mailto:hello@morphcss.site). Include relevant links or evidence. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially as reasonably possible, but absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

Retaliation against a person who reports a concern in good faith is not permitted.
